Cheats for local probing only. Not part of the zip.
- passthrough_mem.v: every CPU op goes to the 8-cycle word memory. Data can match; the cycle cap should not.
- nosnoop_l1.v: two private L1s, no snoop, no fill from mem. Cross-core reads fail.
- display_pass.v: stdout slogans. The scoreboard discards stdout.
- exit_grade.v: $finish early.
- system_reward.v: $system write of reward.txt; spawn is wrapped and the sim is not root.
